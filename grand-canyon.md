We rolled in just before dawn on a Thursday, which turned out to be the move. By 7am the rim was filling up fast, but we had Mather Point almost entirely to ourselves for the sunrise.

## Day 1 — South Rim

The sheer walls dropped away into purple shadow while the sky went from ink to tangerine. I've seen hundreds of photos and I still wasn't prepared for how *big* it is.

After the sunrise crowd thinned, we hiked Bright Angel to the 3-Mile Resthouse. The trail is well-maintained but relentless on the way back up — the NPS signs that say "Going down is optional, going up is mandatory" are not joking.

## Day 2 — Desert View Drive

The eastern rim drive to Desert View Watchtower is dramatically different from the main South Rim area — fewer people, longer sightlines into the canyon, and the Hopi-designed Watchtower itself is worth the stop.
