## Planning Notes

Zion is on the list for the **Utah Five Parks** trip I'm planning for fall 2025.

Key things to sort out before going:

1. Enter the **Angels Landing permit lottery** at [recreation.gov](https://www.recreation.gov) — lotteries run 1–3 months in advance
2. Book lodging in Springdale (gateway town) — fills up fast
3. Decide: Narrows top-down (requires shuttle + camping) or bottom-up (day hike, no permit)

### Possible Itinerary
- Day 1: Arrive, Riverside Walk, Pa'rus Trail to shake off road fatigue
- Day 2: Angels Landing (permit dependent)
- Day 3: The Narrows (bottom-up)
- Day 4: Emerald Pools, drive to Bryce
