## Overview

Ten days looping through the Southwest hitting Arizona, southern Utah, and a quick Nevada detour through Valley of Fire. The plan was loose — book lodging 2–3 nights ahead — which turned out to be perfect.

## Lessons Learned

Start drives early — the light is better and you beat the parking rush. Bring a paper map as backup; cell service in canyon country is basically zero.
